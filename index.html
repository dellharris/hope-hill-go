A Safe, Modern Hope-Hill For Every Child

My principles as a Hope-Hill parent, GO Team Vice Chair and Cluster Representative

I'm Dell Harris, Jude's dad and a Hope-Hill parent who is invested in this school every single day. I ran for the GO Team to bring our families together around what matters most, and my fellow members trusted me as Vice Chair and Cluster Representative.

I've served as Technology Chair at Grant Park Elementary, I'm a community organizer with deep roots in Atlanta, and I build programs and partnerships for a living. What I bring to Hope-Hill is simple: one focus, and the willingness to see it through.

Dell Harris

My Principles

1

Safety for our children comes first

Every decision starts with one question: are our children safe, from the walk to the door to the moment they go home?

2

Clean air and healthy facilities

Children learn best in a building with quality air and safe, well maintained spaces. Families deserve to know that it is tested and confirmed.

3

A playground that is truly theirs

Expand the playground that was built into a fully enclosed space, so children can play freely, protected from outside elements like people walking up and dog waste.

4

A full renovation, not another patch

Patching has not worked. Hope-Hill should be modern and up to date, with a complete renovation that serves this community for decades.

5

A school worthy of where it stands

Hope-Hill is nestled in the Martin Luther King Jr. National Historic Park. Our children deserve a building that honors that legacy.
